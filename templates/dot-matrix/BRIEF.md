## Intent

The full product in the owner's order (deanonymize; engage on site with the rep informed; email and LinkedIn after the visitor leaves; the agent persisting over a long horizon, watching signals; until a meeting is booked), told abstractly. No people, screens, inboxes, desks or org charts: only dots, colour, rhythm and light ("more abstracted away", "more minimal less texty").

## The world

- **The grid:** the whole frame is a dark halftone of tiny dim violet dots (`#140b3c` ground). Every element is made by the dots themselves growing, brightening and changing colour. Nothing else is drawn.
- **The visitor:** a bloom of dots. Grey while anonymous; pink (`#ff5ad1`) once seen.
- **Breakout:** a violet bloom (`#4c00ff` to `#be79ff`). It speaks in ripples: rings of dots that swell outward.
- **The rep:** a small cyan bloom (`#68e6ff`).
- **Signals:** single dots that flash white at the edge of the grid.

## Words

- At most 5 small captions, each the headline of its use-case page, verbatim (getbreakout.ai/use-cases, saved 2026-10-09).
- The dots themselves spell "Meeting booked." and the close, "Pageview to *pipeline.*" (homepage), as dot-matrix type.
- No names, companies or numbers; nothing to label as illustrative.

## Motion

Snappy: nothing glides. Every move is a quick snap (about 0.2 s, hard ease out) landing on a beat of the 96 BPM swing, then a hold. Dots pop rather than fade.

## Sound

Jazzy and atmospheric, free first (General MIDI soundfont): a Rhodes and a warm pad, brushed drums, a walking upright bass, vibraphone for Breakout's ripples and a muted trumpet for the visitor's replies. The band cuts dead when the visitor leaves; the bass alone carries the wait; the full band returns when she comes back.

## Easter egg

While Breakout waits, it turns into the old phone game Snake: a line of dots that slithers across the grid on the beat, eating each signal (one 8-bit blip each) and growing, with a tiny dot-matrix "SCORE" counting up in the corner. When the visitor comes back, the snake is caught mid-game and snaps back into a bloom. The joke: Breakout plays the long game, literally. The game is shown only as the generic idea, with no brand, logo or name on screen.

## Captions

Small and precise: Manrope, 26 px, bottom left, white, a small pink dot before the line. Each cuts in on a downbeat and cuts out on a downbeat, with no fades.
