# Storyboard: piper-pilot v4, "Kickoff"

**Status:** v4 dialogue, awaiting the owner's sign-off. The cast and the look were approved on 2026-10-27 ("okay let's create"); the script was rewritten after the first animatic ("this is terrible, not sounding natural at all"), in the tone of the classic two-person computer ads ("Make it sound like the pc/mac joke tone"). The Pied Piper opener was cut ("breakout comes off as braggy in the opener, skip that. Let's jump directly to 'Big week. Kickoff.'").

## Tone

- **Piper gets the jokes and doesn't know it.** Proud, earnest, a little stuffy; cheerfully describes a process that sounds painful.
- **Breakout is nice.** Relaxed, polite, a little amused; mentions how easy things are, never brags, never insults.
- **Understated.** Short lines, pauses, nobody raises their voice. One premise, a prop (Piper's blank binder), a polite last line from Piper.
- The tone of the genre only: no lines, props, music, casting or framing from Apple's campaign.
