## Tone

- **Piper gets the jokes and doesn't know it.** Proud, earnest, a little stuffy; cheerfully describes a process that sounds painful.
- **Breakout is nice.** Relaxed, polite, a little amused; mentions how easy things are, never brags, never insults.
- **Understated.** Short lines, pauses, nobody raises their voice. One premise, a prop (Piper's blank binder), a polite last line from Piper.
- The tone of the genre only: no lines, props, music, casting or framing from Apple's campaign.
