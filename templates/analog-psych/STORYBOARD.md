# Storyboard: Breakout Plays

**Status:** approved by the owner on 2026-10-05; the first cut is built in `templates/analog-psych/`. Defaults taken for the open questions: the swarm concept, 16:9, engraved typographic hook (nothing paid), a free scratch bed, Frame 12 as type only (no meeting screen yet), the sequencer framed out, and "Watch signals, *not lists.*" to close.

This film tells demand gen and RevOps leaders that **any signal can start a play: Breakout researches the account, finds the whole buying committee, writes the email and books the meeting.**

| Length | 20 bars at 110 BPM (1 bar = 2.18 s, 1 beat = 0.55 s): 43.6 s |

## The fact, and its sources

Scout bees find candidate sites and advertise them with waggle dances. Other scouts visit, and the best site wins more dancers. When about 15 scouts are present at one site at the same time, they sense the quorum and trigger the swarm's move. Cornell called it "decision-making to rival any department committee".

- Thomas D. Seeley, Cornell: [news.cornell.edu](https://news.cornell.edu/node/276432), [American Scientist, "Group Decision Making in Honey Bee Swarms"](https://www.americanscientist.org/article/evolution-religion-and-free-will), [AskNature, "Quorum determines new hive site"](https://asknature.org/strategy/quorum-determines-new-hive-site/)
- On screen we say "about 15", as the research does.

## Differentiation test

1. **Logo swap:** the swarm and quorum idea is tied to a product feature you can see (Visitor Count ≥ 3, Contacts per Company 3). A competitor could borrow the metaphor, but not these screens or the 700+ signals line.
2. **The world:** the site's violet mesh and serif with italic key words carry the body. The hexagon links the paper world to the product.
3. **The outcome:** a booked meeting. That frame still needs a real screen.

## Alternative concept: the first spam (if the swarm is not right)

May 3, 1978: a computer-company marketer emailed 393 ARPANET users at once, the first documented spam ([Abusix](https://abusix.com/blog/email-security/the-birth-of-email-spam-gary-thuerks-1978-green-card-incident/), [CAUCE](https://cauce.org/?p=135)). Outbound has been the same blast ever since. Plays is the opposite: one signal, one account, an email that knows why it is writing. Same body from Frame 5 on; the hook becomes a terminal and teletype world instead of a field guide. No company is named on screen.

## Open questions

1. **Concept:** the swarm (recommended) or the first spam?
2. **Destination and aspect:** where will this run? 16:9 for YouTube and the site, and 4:5 for the LinkedIn feed?
3. **The meeting screen (Frame 12):** can you send a screenshot of a booked meeting from a play, or should the film end on Test and the lockup?
4. **The sequencer's name** is visible on the Destination screen. Keep it legible (it names a real integration), or frame past it?
5. **Closing line:** "Watch signals, not lists." or "Pageview to pipeline."? Should "Plays" be the only title?
6. **The hook picture:** stay typographic and engraved (free, with diagrams that carry meaning), or generate macro bee footage in a Super 8 look? That is paid: about 3 credits a second at 480p for drafts, and about 12 a second at 1080p. 3 shots of 4 s at 1080p is about 150 credits, roughly $7.50 at the 2026-09 rate.
7. **Music:** a free scratch bed for the first cut, or 2 or 3 generated psych-indie takes on your account (about 2 to 3 credits each)? Or do you have a licensed track?
8. **The numbers:** "700+ signals" comes from the homepage. Is there any other figure for Plays you can prove and want shown?
