# Storyboard: "Downtown missions" (DRAFT)

**Status:** built as a draft at the owner's request ("I want you to build the game simulation"); the open questions in BRIEF.md still stand. 44.3 s at 92 BPM (1 bar = 2.61 s), 17 bars. As built, the tail runs Monday dusk on Market, Wednesday night behind her cable car up the hill, and Friday morning across from her new office; the light follows the game clock. A G-funk bed throughout, our own composition. Every company and person is fictional and labelled "Illustrative example".

## Decisions to make first

- One film or two cuts (each mission alone is about 22 s; bars 0 to 1 and 15 to 17 wrap each one).
- The champion signal's exact wording, once the claim is confirmed.
- Which close line.

## Frame 1: Downtown, the radar wakes

- bars: 0 to 1.5 (0 to 3.9 s)
- scene: Low-poly aerial over downtown SF at golden hour, smog haze, the camera drifting down Market Street. The HUD clicks on piece by piece: radar bottom left (Breakout's b-disc rides its rim), clock, health bar and wanted stars top right. The radar's b-disc lights violet.
- continuity_in: the camera already moving from frame 1
- continuity_out: the radar sweeps; a blip appears
- signature: world-pass
- sound: city ambience, a radar sweep, the bass and drums kick in on bar 1
- words: none

## Frame 2: Mission 1 title

- bars: 1.5 to 3 (3.9 to 7.8 s)
- scene: 3 blips flare in one tower on the radar, then turn violet. Cut to the tower in the world; 3 violet markers glow in its windows. Big outlined title slams in: "Pizza for the Buying Committee". Small: "Illustrative example: Northwind, 3 visitors, 1 office".
- continuity_in: the blip on the radar becomes the tower in the world (radar push-in)
- continuity_out: the markers pulse, a timer bar appears
- signature: macro-pull-back (in reverse: radar into world)
- sound: 3 radar pings, the mission-start whoosh on the title
- words: (on screen) "3+ visitors from one office."

## Frame 3: Before it cools

- bars: 3 to 4 (7.8 to 10.4 s)
- scene: The hero grabs a stack of pizza boxes from a generic corner shop (no brand) and hops on a moped. Yellow objective text: "Get there before the signal cools." A heat bar on the HUD starts draining from violet to grey.
- continuity_in: the heat bar from the title
- continuity_out: the moped launching out of frame
- sound: box thump, moped rev
- words: "Get there before the signal cools."

## Frame 4: The run

- bars: 4 to 6.5 (10.4 to 17 s)
- scene: The ride down Market and over a hill, a jump off the crest, a cable car dodged, all in quick hard cuts on the beat. The heat bar keeps draining; the radar shows the tower getting closer.
- continuity_in: the moped already at speed
- continuity_out: braking at the tower's door
- signature: world-pass
- sound: engine, a tyre squeal on the downbeat, the lead synth enters
- words: none

## Frame 5: Mission passed

- bars: 6.5 to 8 (17 to 20.9 s)
- scene: The hero walks in with the pizzas; the 3 violet markers gather around them. The heat bar stops with violet left. Big outlined text: "MISSION PASSED" (wording to confirm), under it "Meeting *booked.*"
- continuity_in: the door opening
- continuity_out: the text slams out; the clock on the HUD starts spinning
- sound: our own brass and synth sting, a beat of silence before it
- words: "Meeting booked."

## Frame 6: Mission 2 title

- bars: 8 to 9.5 (20.9 to 24.8 s)
- scene: The HUD clock spins; a pager buzzes on screen. One blip on the radar, the champion, flips from one building to another across town. Title: "Tail the Champion". Small: "Illustrative example: Maya R., your champion at Northwind".
- continuity_in: the spinning clock
- continuity_out: the blip moving
- sound: pager buzz, mission-start whoosh
- words: (on screen) the signal line, to confirm (e.g. "Your champion just changed jobs.")

## Frame 7: Don't lose them

- bars: 9.5 to 13 (24.8 to 33.9 s)
- scene: The champion leaves the old office carrying a box. Yellow text: "Follow them. Don't get too close." A distance meter on the HUD. The hero follows on foot (Monday dusk), then on the moped behind her cable car up the hill (Wednesday night), then watches from across the street (Friday morning): the HUD clock jumps and the light follows it. The violet blip never leaves the radar: Breakout keeps tracking. Each time the meter says "Too close", the hero eases off.
- continuity_in: the blip on the radar becomes the champion in the street
- continuity_out: the champion walking up to a new tower
- signature: branch-and-pulse (the radar pulse every bar, Breakout listening)
- sound: a slower groove, a radar ping every bar, footsteps, the clock tick on each day jump
- words: "Follow them. Don't get too close."

## Frame 8: The new office

- bars: 13 to 15 (33.9 to 39.1 s)
- scene: The champion enters the new building ("Illustrative example: Halcyon"). Their blip turns violet in the new tower: a new account, a known friend. Big outlined text: "MISSION PASSED", under it "New account. Same champion."
- continuity_in: the tower doors
- continuity_out: the camera rises above the city
- sound: the sting again, varied
- words: "New account. Same champion." (draft, to approve)

## Frame 9: Close

- bars: 15 to 17 (39.1 to 44.4 s)
- scene: The camera pulls up over downtown; the whole radar fills with violet markers, every one a mission. The HUD falls away; the Breakout wordmark (logo-on-dark) and the close line in near silence.
- continuity_in: the rising camera
- continuity_out: hold
- signature: macro-pull-back, logo-build
- sound: the bed drops out, one last synth note, near silence under the logo
- words: close line from brand.json copy (to choose)
