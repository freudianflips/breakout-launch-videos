## The look: the genre, not the game

`docs/rights.md` lets a film use a game's era and genre, never the game, its characters or its brand. So: a low-poly city with haze and a warm grade, a round radar, clock, health bar and wanted stars of our own design, outlined display type in open fonts, letterboxed cutscenes, a G-funk bed of our own. Never: the game's name, logo, CJ or any character or likeness, its places, typefaces, sting or music.

- **The hero** is fictional: a violet jacket (Breakout's colour, muted), a cap, jeans, white sneakers. Name to agree.
- **Downtown SF** as generic low-poly geography: Market Street with palms, the hills with flat intersections, cable cars, a white spire, a clock tower at the foot of Market, a bridge in the haze. No real company's signage.
- **Breakout in the world:** the radar is Breakout (its north badge is the b-disc mark); signals are violet (blips, the 3 windows, the arrows over targets, the beams at the close). The close is the real wordmark.

## Sound

No narrator. A G-funk bed in C minor at 92 BPM, synthesised (FM electric piano, gliding sub bass, kick, clap, swung hats, a portamento sine lead), a beat of silence and our own sting for each mission passed, half-time drums for the tail, the bed out under the logo. Effects: a moped engine that follows the ride, a cable car bell, radar pings, a pager, a tyre squeal, a city bed. -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
