# Cast stills

Character options for the pilot, generated on Higgsfield Soul 2.0 (`text2image_soul_v2`, 3:4, 2k) on 2026-10-27. Prompts in `prompts.json`; job ids, seeds and result URLs in `jobs.json`; `contact.jpg` is the review sheet. The full-size PNGs stay out of git (download them again from the URLs in `jobs.json`).

All people are generated and fictional. Before a still is chosen, check its face for a likeness to anyone famous, and to Qualified's staff or Piper's own marketing character; regenerate rather than fix.
