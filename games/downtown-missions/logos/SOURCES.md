# Target account logos

The real logos of the companies in the game, saved from each company's own homepage on 2026-10-21 (class names and titles stripped; Anthropic's and Rippling's `currentColor` set to their ink colours). They belong to their owners and appear only as the signs on each company's building, on the booking screen and, for Waymo, on its cars, at the owner's request.
