# Storyline: Plays, "Signal in. Meeting out."

**Status:** draft v2 for the owner's confirmation. Nothing is built until it is confirmed.

**The film tells** demand gen and RevOps leaders one thing: **any signal goes in, a booked meeting comes out.** In the middle, one example play runs. You watch the agent read the account, think, write an email from 2 signals (a pricing-page visit and a new exec hire), get approved and send.

| Sound | No narrator. A minimal, dry electronic track (kick, click, sub). Soft key-clicks as text types, a tick on each "Act", a chime on Approved. Near silence for the lockup. |

## Changes from v1

- Bars 3 to 7 are now one example play shown through the agent cards, in place of 4 separate UI macros.
- The email is the hero: you see it written from 2 named signals, with lines showing which sentence came from which signal.
- The approval step from your references is in, so it's clear a human stays in the loop.
- The references' third-party names (the company, the gifting and social tools) are replaced: a fictional account, and no tool brands.
- About 2 s longer (17 s), to give the email room to read.
