# Storyboard: Plays, "Press play."

**Status:** approved by the owner on 2026-10-05 ("Create it. 16:9 first."). Defaults taken for the open questions: "Listen." to open, "Plays" + ▶ to close, the Notion example, 16:9 first. Built in `film/press-play/`.

This film tells demand gen and RevOps leaders that **your signals are the notes: press play, and Breakout turns them into a booked meeting.**

## Decisions

| | |
|---|---|
| Concept | **Press play.** A step sequencer on the Swiss grid. Signals are rows, lit steps are hits, the agent arranges them into a beat, and ▶ is the drop. |
| Spine | Watch it run: signals go in, the play builds, ▶, the email writes, the meeting lands, the lockup |
| Length | 15 bars at 128 BPM (1 bar = 1.875 s, 1 beat = 0.469 s): **28.1 s** |
| Act map | Intro bars 1–2 · build bars 3–8 · drop on bar 9 · burst bars 9–12 (53–80 %) · outro bars 13–15 (20 %, the beat strips away) |
| Voice | None. On-screen words only, at most 3 words a line. |
| Music | Dry, minimal electronic made from the pattern itself: kick, clap, hats, a sub and a 3-note chord for the committee. A riser into the drop and near silence on the last bar. |
| Look | Look test 03: off-white `#f2f1ee`, hairline grid, mono indexes, Manrope 800. The sequencer's off steps are hairline squares and the lit steps are solid violet. One violet flood at the meeting. |
| Signature | Picture and sound locked 1:1, the mute, and the ▶ that turns into ✓ |

## Frame 1: Listen

- bars: 1–2 (0.0–3.75 s)
- scene: The grid is already drawn, an empty 16-step sequencer with 4 blank rows. A thin violet playhead sweeps from frame 1. On bar 2's downbeat, "**Listen.**" slams in huge, top-left, and the index `01 / Signal` appears in mono.
- transition_in: none. The film opens with the playhead already moving.
- continuity_out: the playhead keeps sweeping into bar 3.
- signature: none
- sound: only the closed hat, as the grid's clock. A soft tick each time the playhead crosses a column.
- words: Listen.

## Frame 2: The first notes

- bars: 3–4 (3.75–7.5 s)
- scene: "Listen." slides out of frame. Row 1's label types on, `Pricing page viewed 3×`, and its 4 steps light as the kick enters. On bar 4, row 2 types `New CFO hired`, and the clap enters on its 2 steps. Small mono `Notion · Illustrative example` sits in the corner.
- transition_in: the word's exit and the first lit step land on the same downbeat.
- continuity_out: the 2 rows keep playing; row 3's label starts typing.
- signature: picture–sound lock
- sound: kick, then clap, added on the downbeats. Key ticks as the labels type.
- words: none (the row labels are the words)

## Frame 3: The mute

- bars: 5 (7.5–9.4 s)
- scene: Row 3, `Return visit`, adds the off-beat hats. Row 4, `2 blog views`, flickers in at low opacity as a messy ghost pattern. On beat 3, its **M** button presses, the row greys out and the tag reads `No action`. Rows 1–3 tag `Act` in violet, 1 per beat.
- transition_in: continuous. The same grid keeps playing.
- continuity_out: the camera starts pulling back.
- signature: the mute
- sound: hats enter. The ghost shaker plays for 2 beats, then cuts dead on the mute with a dry click.
- words: Act · No action

## Frame 4: 700+

- bars: 6 (9.4–11.25 s)
- scene: A fast pull back. The 4 rows turn out to be a tiny part of a huge sequencer: hundreds of thin rows, mostly dark, a few flickering. The counter runs to **700+ signals**.
- transition_in: the pull back starts already moving from Frame 3.
- continuity_out: the camera snaps back into the 4 rows.
- signature: macro pull-back
- sound: a filtered wash of many tiny ticks under the beat, then a snap back to the dry beat.
- words: 700+ signals

## Frame 5: Press play

- bars: 7–8 (11.25–15.0 s)
- scene: Back on the 4 rows. On bar 7, the playhead stops and everything holds still: the riser starts. On bar 8, a big **▶** slams into the centre cell with "**Press play.**" beside it and the index `02 / Play`. On the last beat of bar 8, a cursor arrives and the button depresses.
- transition_in: the hard stop of the playhead, on the downbeat.
- continuity_out: ▶ is down. On the downbeat, its violet floods into the email's cell.
- signature: the ▶
- sound: the beat drops out, leaving a riser and the sub swell. A click on the press, then 1 beat of silence before the drop.
- words: Press play.

## Frame 6: The email, on the beat

- bars: 9–10 (15.0–18.75 s)
- scene: **The drop.** The whole beat returns, with the committee chord added. The email card fills the right half. The subject "A cost breakdown for your new CFO" lands on the downbeat. Then "Congrats on bringing on a new CFO." types, with a violet line drawn from the `New CFO hired` row on its clap. "...on our pricing page a few times this week" types, with a line from the `Pricing page viewed 3×` row on its kick. Each phrase lands on a step. Mono cuts 2 a beat: `Browsing history` · `Firmographics` · `AI enrichment`.
- transition_in: the violet from ▶ opens into the card.
- continuity_out: the To field fans out into 3 recipients.
- signature: picture–sound lock (every phrase sits on a hit)
- sound: the full beat. Key ticks are quantised to 16ths.
- words: the subject and the 2 personalised lines

## Frame 7: The committee, sent

- bars: 11 (18.75–20.6 s)
- scene: The To field fans out into 3 chips, **CFO · Head of RevOps · VP Marketing**, each lit on 1 note of the chord. `Buying committee` appears in mono. On beat 4, the card swipes off to the right with "**Sent.**"
- transition_in: continuous from the email.
- continuity_out: the swipe leaves the ▶ button alone in the centre.
- signature: none
- sound: the 3-note chord, 1 note per chip. A whoosh on the send.
- words: Buying committee · Sent.

## Frame 8: Meeting booked

- bars: 12 (20.6–22.5 s)
- scene: On the downbeat, violet floods out of the ▶ to fill the frame. The ▶ redraws itself into a big ✓, with "**Meeting booked.**" and the booked card: `Thu, Oct 15 · 2:00 PM` with 3 attendee dots. This is the success state from signal v3, tighter.
- transition_in: the flood, starting from the button.
- continuity_out: the beat starts stripping. Kick and clap drop out on the last beat.
- signature: ▶ → ✓
- sound: the biggest hit of the film (sub drop and logo-sting family), then the beat thins.
- words: Meeting booked.

## Frame 9: Lockup

- bars: 13–15 (22.5–28.1 s)
- scene: The violet recedes to the off-white grid, with 1 row left: a single playhead ticking. Centred, the wordmark, then "**Plays**" with a small ▶ beside it, then `getbreakout.ai` in mono. The playhead stops on the last bar.
- transition_in: the flood draws back into the ▶ beside "Plays".
- continuity_out: the end. The playhead stops on the final downbeat.
- signature: logo-build
- sound: only the hat, then 1 soft tick, then silence for the last 2 beats.
- words: Plays · getbreakout.ai

## Differentiation test

1. **Logo swap:** another vendor couldn't swap their logo in without losing the meaning. The pun only works for a product called *Plays*. The mute shows that the agent picks which signals to act on, and the ▶ is the product's own name.
2. **The world:** the approved Swiss grid becomes the sequencer's grid. It isn't decoration; it is the instrument.
3. **The outcome:** the climax is "Meeting booked.", the drawn ✓ and a real slot.
