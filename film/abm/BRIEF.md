## Intent

This is a series of short ABM films, one per account, for companies whose website chat runs on Qualified. Each film opens on the account's own homepage with its chat widget open. It shows that this widget offers buttons and no text box. It then shows where Breakout outperforms Qualified, and closes on the contract buyout.

The owner asked to "speak to the fact that the qualified chatbot is a pure decision tree bot. You can't ask it open text questions." Our own compare page lists Qualified with "AI Chat… Yes". So the film makes the point about each account's capture ("kornferry.com today: 4 buttons, no text box."), not about Qualified in general. Check this line against every new capture.

**Audience:** marketing and demand gen leaders at those accounts.
**Tone:** the approved Swiss grid and the orchestral score from "Press play.". Challenger and confident, but every comparison line is quoted, never paraphrased into a stronger claim.

## Assets

- **Per account:** a homepage capture with the chat widget open, at about 2000 px wide (`accounts/<slug>/site.webp`), and `account.json`.
- **Breakout's widget**, rebuilt in code from the real screens (`film/assets/screens/agent-welcome.png`, `agent-question.png`). It uses the real copy "Go ahead. Ask me stuff." and "Book a Meeting", and the avatar is cropped from the screen.
- **From the Plays films:** the agent card style, the email card and the "Meeting booked." success state.
