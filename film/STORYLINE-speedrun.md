## Look: over the top, on brand

The base is moodboard tile 01, "Strobe type", pushed further.

| Lever | Choice |
|---|---|
| Grounds | Hard flips on the beat between near-black `#040405`, white and electric violet `#4c00ff`. 3 grounds, maximum contrast, no gradients. |
| Pop colour | Lilac `#be79ff` on black and violet for the one thing that matters per shot (the ticked split, the live signal). Option: a hot pink pop as well, see question 3. |
| Type | Manrope 800, very tight tracking, words at 600 to 1000 px that crop off the frame edges. Speed-run HUD in Fragment Mono. |
| Motion | Hard cuts on every beat, 2 cuts a beat in the burst. Words slam from 2.4x with a 2-frame motion blur. Speed lines streak toward the vanishing point between splits. A 4 to 8 px screen shake on downbeats. An RGB split on the 4 biggest slams only. |
| HUD | Stays on screen throughout, so the eye has an anchor while everything else flips. Top left: the Breakout b-mark as the "player" icon. Top right: the splits list, 6 rows, each ticking ✓ in lilac. Bottom: a progress bar, 0/6 to 6/6. |
| Logo | The mark in the HUD for the whole run. The wordmark, untouched, as a 2-frame cut-in on 3 downbeats (bars 2, 4 and 5). The full lockup at the end. Always its own colours: `logo.svg` on white, `logo-on-dark.svg` on black and violet, never recoloured. |
| Product | The approved agent cards and email from the signal film, reused as macro fragments of 0.5 to 1 s, plus the v3 "Meeting booked." success state |
| Flash safety | No more than 3 full-frame light/dark flips a second (the photosensitive-epilepsy limit, WCAG 2.3.1). The 2-cuts-a-beat burst changes the type and keeps the ground. |

This breaks 3 of the house rules on purpose: "glitch, chromatic split", "flash" and "pure black and white" are on the avoid list in `skills/launch-motion` and `references/lessons.md`. The owner asked for over the top, so we try it in frames first and log the verdict.

## Built for the first 10 seconds

- **Frame 0 is already moving.** No logo card or title card first. The film opens on "GO." mid-slam with speed lines already streaking.
- **An open loop by 0.5 s.** The HUD shows 6 empty splits and a 0/6 bar. The viewer stays to see it fill.
- **Something changes at least every 0.47 s** (every beat), with a bigger change on every downbeat.
- **The payoff lands before 10 s:** "Meeting booked." at 9.4 s, 6/6, "Run complete."
- **Reads muted:** every split is named on screen, and sound adds punch but never carries meaning.
- **Brand from frame 0:** the mark is in the HUD for the whole run, so even a 3-second viewer sees whose film it is.
